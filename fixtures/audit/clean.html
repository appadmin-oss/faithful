Twelve plots free this spring

Our society has looked after the Hollins Lane site since 1952. Each plot is ten rods, with a water trough at the end of every row and a shared shed for tools. Rent is £34 a year, payable at the April meeting.

We ask new members to work their plot at least twice a month through the growing season, and to keep paths clear. The committee walks the site in June and September and will write to anyone whose plot has gone to seed.

How to apply

  • Fill in the form at the hut on any Saturday morning.
  • Bring proof of address within the parish.
  • Pay the £10 key deposit when you are offered a plot.
